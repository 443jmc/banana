## **Why Couples Choose to Work With Me**

I use a differentiation-based approach, grounded in the work of David Schnarch. This isn't the typical "learn to communicate better" therapy that leaves most couples feeling like they wasted their time.

Instead, we focus on helping each of you grow as individuals. When two people become more solid in themselves—more able to handle discomfort, more willing to be honest—the relationship transforms.

Most couples come in hoping I'll fix their partner. What they discover is that the path to a better relationship runs through their own growth.

## **What I Help With**

**Communication problems** — You're talking, but nothing gets through. The same arguments repeat. I'll help you break the pattern.

**Disconnection** — You feel more like roommates than partners. The spark is gone. I'll help you find your way back to each other.

**Infidelity** — Trust has been shattered. Whether you're the one who strayed or the one who was betrayed, healing is possible.

**Constant conflict** — Every conversation turns into a fight. I'll help you understand what's really driving the tension.

**Considering separation** — You're not sure if the relationship can be saved. I'll help you find clarity.
