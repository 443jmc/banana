## **What Is Relationship Coaching?**

Coaching is forward-focused. Instead of treating mental health conditions, we work on specific goals: improving communication, navigating a difficult decision, strengthening your connection, or figuring out what you actually want from your relationship.

Think of it like working with a skilled guide. I bring decades of experience helping couples grow. You bring the willingness to look honestly at yourself and your relationship. Together, we figure out what's not working and what to do about it.

Coaching works well for couples and individuals who:

*   Want to improve a good relationship and make it great
    
*   Are facing a specific challenge (career change, relocation, blending families)
    
*   Feel stuck in patterns but don't have a mental health diagnosis
    
*   Want practical tools and direct feedback
    
*   Live outside California and can't access my therapy services
    

## **How Coaching Differs From Therapy**

This distinction matters, so let me be clear:

**Outside of California, I do not:**

*   Diagnose mental health conditions
    
*   Treat psychological disorders
    
*   Provide therapy or counseling
    
*   Create treatment plans for mental illness
    
*   Provide documentation for insurance reimbursement
    

**Coaching is not covered by insurance.** Because coaching is not a healthcare service, you cannot submit sessions for reimbursement from your health insurance provider.

**As a coach, I do:**

*   Help you clarify what you want
    
*   Offer direct observations about patterns I see
    
*   Teach practical skills for communication and connection
    
*   Challenge you to grow beyond your comfort zone
    
*   Hold you accountable to your own goals
    

If you're experiencing depression, anxiety, trauma, or other mental health concerns, therapy is the appropriate service. I offer therapy to clients located in California. For clients outside California, I offer coaching.

## **My Approach**

My coaching draws on the same principles I use in my therapy practice—particularly the concept of **differentiation**: becoming solid enough in yourself that you can be truly close to another person without losing yourself in them.

Most relationship problems come down to two people who haven't fully developed themselves trying to get the other person to change. Coaching helps you focus on the only person you can actually control: yourself.

We'll work on developing what I call a **quiet mind and calm heart**—the ability to manage your own emotions without needing your partner to soothe you, and without trying to manage theirs.

This isn't about learning communication tricks or conflict resolution scripts. It's about becoming someone who can handle the truth about yourself and your relationship.

## **How It Works**

**Sessions** are 50 minutes via secure video. We meet weekly or biweekly, depending on your goals and schedule.

**Between sessions**, you'll have specific things to practice or reflect on. Coaching works best when you're actively engaged in the process, not just showing up for sessions.

**Timeline** varies. Some clients come for a few sessions to work through a specific issue. Others work with me for several months on deeper patterns. We'll figure out what makes sense for your situation.

## **Is Coaching Right for You?**

Coaching is a good fit if:

*   You're motivated to do the work, not just talk about problems
    
*   You want direct feedback, even when it's uncomfortable
    
*   You're focused on growth rather than symptom relief
    
*   You're willing to look at your own role in your relationship dynamics
    
*   You live outside California and want to work with me
    

Coaching may not be the right fit if:

*   You're in crisis or experiencing severe mental health symptoms
    
*   You need a diagnosis or treatment for a mental health condition
    
*   You're looking for someone to validate that your partner is the problem
    
*   You want insurance to cover your sessions
    

If you're unsure which service is right for you, reach out and we can discuss your situation.
