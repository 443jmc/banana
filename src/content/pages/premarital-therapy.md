# **Premarital Therapy in Roseville, CA**

**You're about to make a huge commitment,** and you want to make sure you and your partner are on the same page. That's where premarital counseling comes in!

**Premarital counseling is like a check-up for your relationship.** It's a chance to talk about important topics, learn some skills, and build a strong foundation for your marriage. And it can help you have a **more passionate and fulfilling relationship**.

**Here’s how I can help you in premarital counseling:**

*   **Learn to handle those tricky in-law situations.** Let’s be honest, sometimes families can be a bit much. In premarital counseling, we’ll figure out how to set healthy boundaries with your families and support each other so you can avoid those big blow-ups.
    
*   **Become a pro at disagreeing (in a good way).** Conflict is normal in any relationship, but it doesn’t have to turn into a screaming match. You’ll learn how to **communicate effectively, stay calm, and work through disagreements together**.
    
*   **Figure out what you really want (and how to ask for it).** Being able to express your needs and desires is a superpower in a relationship. We'll work on **open and honest communication** so you can feel comfortable sharing your thoughts and feelings with each other.
    
*   **Get that spark back (or keep it burning bright).** Intimacy is a big part of a healthy marriage, and it goes beyond just the physical stuff. We’ll talk about **building emotional intimacy, exploring your sexual desires, and creating a fulfilling and passionate connection**.
    

Think of premarital counseling as an investment in your happiness. It's a chance to learn some valuable skills and set yourselves up for a **lifetime of love and connection**! If you’re ready to take the plunge, I’m here to guide you every step of the way.
