## **What Brings Rocklin Couples to Therapy**

The couples I work with are dealing with the same challenges you might be facing:

*   Communication has broken down and the same arguments keep repeating
    
*   One or both partners feel disconnected or lonely in the relationship
    
*   Trust has been damaged by infidelity or deception
    
*   You're stuck in a pattern and don't know how to break out of it
    
*   Life transitions (new baby, job change, empty nest) have strained the relationship
    

Whatever brought you here, the goal is the same: building a relationship where both partners feel seen, respected, and connected.

## **My Approach**

I use a differentiation-based approach, grounded in the work of David Schnarch. Instead of just teaching communication skills, I help each partner develop a stronger sense of self within the relationship.

When two people grow individually, the relationship transforms. You stop needing your partner to change and start focusing on who you want to become.

## **Session Options**

**Regular sessions** are 50 minutes, scheduled weekly or biweekly. These work well for ongoing growth and maintenance.

**Intensive sessions** are three to four hours per day, spread across one to three days. These are ideal if you want faster progress or are in crisis.

Both formats are available in person at my Roseville office or via secure video.
