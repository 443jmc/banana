# Collaborative Divorce Resources for Couples in Roseville CA

### Community & Non-Profit Resources

[**Sacramento Collaborative Divorce Group (SCDG)**](https://sacramentocollaborativedivorce.com/)  
A non-profit association since 1997, offering team-based collaborative divorce, free educational workshops, and connections to lawyers, coaches & financial neutrals.

[**Placer Dispute Resolution**](https://www.placerdisputeresolution.org/)  
A low-cost, voluntary, confidential community mediation center in Rocklin—serving the entire county with sliding-scale fees for divorce, parenting, and property issues.

[**Placer County Self-Help Center (Roseville Courthouse)**](https://www.placer.ca.gov/1483/Dispute-Resolution)  
Free legal self-help for pro se divorce filers, form assistance, workshops, and referrals to low-cost resources, based at the Placer County courthouse.

[**Sacramento Family Court Resources (Carol Miller Justice Center)**](https://www.placer.courts.ca.gov/divisions/family/child-custody-mediation)  
Offers court-based mediation, walk-in divorce clinics, and custody support services at 301 Bicentennial Cir., Sacramento.
