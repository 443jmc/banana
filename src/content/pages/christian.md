# **Christian Couples Therapy  
in Roseville CA**

**“Love one another” is easier said than done.**

Do you feel heard and understood in your marriage? Do you feel truly safe when you’re together? Are you walking on eggshells, trying to avoid another argument?

**Bring more love into your home**

I help Christian couples create thriving, faithful marriages. Along with traditional marriage therapy, I also offer intensive sessions to help you overcome deeply entrenched problems.

**Facing the hard stuff**

I work with neurodiversity, narcissism, deception, gaslighting, manipulation, sexuality, [infidelity](/infidelity), betrayal, and trauma. We’ll explore the root causes of your relationship distress during your first session, and talk about what you can do to make things better.

**Christian Sexuality Counseling**

Are you concerned about pornography, infidelity, emotional affairs, or sexual addiction? Does your marriage suffer from a mismatch in sexual desire? I have helped many Christian couples overcome sexual challenges in marriage. You too can enjoy God’s gift of sexuality in your marriage.
