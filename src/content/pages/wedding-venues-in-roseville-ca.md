# Indoor Wedding Venues near Roseville CA

### [Maidu Community Center](https://www.roseville.ca.us/government/departments/parks/book_with_us/maidu_community_center_rentals) – City‑run event hall in Roseville

Price (venue‑only): ≈ $170–400 per hour; full‑evening ≈ $1–2 K.  
Capacity: ~200–280 seated (400 standing).  
Summary: Large, neutral banquet hall with kitchen and patio—DIY‑friendly and very budget‑conscious.

### [Whitney Oaks Golf Club](https://www.whitneyoaksgolf.com/weddings-events) – Golf‑course clubhouse in Rocklin

Price: $1,200–2,900 rental (day‑dependent); ceremony lawn $1,500–2,200.  
Capacity: Up to 200.  
Summary: Two connected banquet rooms plus terrace overlooking greens; on‑site catering, oak‑tree ceremony site.

### [Sierra View Country Club](https://www.sierraviewcc.org/) – Private golf club in Roseville

Price: ~$1,500–4,500 rental (waived for members).  
Capacity: ~150 indoors (200+ with outdoor lawn).  
Summary: Elegant clubhouse and “Grove” ceremony space; panoramic course views; in‑house chef.

### [Timber Creek Ballroom](https://timbercreekballroom.com/) – Sun City Roseville

Price: $250–400 per hour (5‑hr min) or $5,000 all‑day Saturday; ceremony add‑on $800–1,000.  
Capacity: 240 seated reception; 300 ceremony in Rose Garden Arbor.  
Summary: Largest ballroom in Roseville, rose‑garden ceremony, tables/linens/staff included.

### [The Clubhouse at Woodcreek](http://clubhousewoodcreek.com/) – City‑owned golf‑course venue

Price: ~ $1,850–2,500 for 50 guests (packages scale up).  
Capacity: Up to 200.  
Summary: Banquet hall with golf‑course vistas, patio ceremony lawn; budget‑friendly packages.

### [Blue Goose Event Center](https://bluegooseeventcenter.com/) – Historic fruit‑packing barn (Loomis)

Price: $1,900 Saturday (12 hrs); weekdays $900–1,650.  
Capacity: 250–300 seated (375 standing).  
Summary: Rustic industrial hall with stage, bar, kitchen; BYO vendors; full‑day rental under $2 K.

### [The Chalet Event Center](http://thechaleteventcenter.com/) – Modern venue in Rocklin

Price: Starting site fee $2,000 (packages available).  
Capacity: Up to 250.  
Summary: Contemporary hall plus courtyard; allows outside vendors or all‑inclusive packages.

### [Rocklin Event Center](https://www.rocklin.ca.us/post/event-center-ballroom) – City complex with ballroom & garden

Price: Saturday package ≈ $3,000 (10 hrs); weekdays lower.  
Capacity: 250+ indoors; garden for 200+.  
Summary: Neutral ballroom, landscaped courtyard ceremony, outside catering allowed.

### [BLOC Venue](https://blocvenue.com/) – Industrial‑chic loft in Roseville

Price: $2,500–6,500 rental range.  
Capacity: Up to 200.  
Summary: 7,000 sq ft blank‑canvas space with exposed brick and beams; built‑in A/V and suites.

### [La Provence Restaurant & Terrace](https://laprovenceroseville.com/events-weddings/) – French‑country restaurant

Price: ≈ $3,000+ (food‑&‑bev minimums).  
Capacity: Up to 180 using all rooms/patios.  
Summary: Garden ceremony meadow, twinkle‑lit terrace, gourmet in‑house cuisine.

### [Best Western Plus Orchid Hotel & Suites](https://www.bestwestern.com/en_US/book/hotel-details.05718.html) – Hotel ballroom

Price: Contact for package pricing (typically F&B minimum).  
Capacity: Up to 300 across divisible rooms.  
Summary: Practical hotel option with onsite lodging; flexible for cultural or large receptions.

### [Hyatt Place Sacramento/Roseville](https://www.hyatt.com/en-US/hotel/california/hyatt-place-sacramento-roseville/smfzr/event-spaces) – Intimate hotel venue

Price: Packages from ≈ $105 pp (catering included).  
Capacity: ~60 guests.  
Summary: Modern meeting room plus patio; straightforward small‑wedding packages near Galleria.

### [Union Brick by Wedgewood Weddings](https://www.wedgewoodweddings.com/unionbrick) – All‑inclusive estate in Roseville

Price: $1,195–5,495 site; ceremony $995; packages from $56 pp.  
Capacity: 300.  
Summary: Waterfall courtyard ceremony and bright ballroom; turnkey Wedgewood packages.

### [Orchard Creek Lodge](https://suncity-lincolnhills.org/orchardcreeklodge/) – Resort‑style lodge (Lincoln)

Price: Packages from ~$7,000.  
Capacity: 300.  
Summary: Grand ballroom with terrace by pond; full in‑house catering and scenic photo spots.

### [Morgan Creek Golf Club](https://morgancreekclub.com/weddings-events-2/) – Private club in Roseville

Price: No hall fee; budgets ~$8–10 K+ (food/bar packages).  
Capacity: 250 indoor (350+ with pavilion).  
Summary: Lakeside gazebo ceremony, vaulted ballroom, Chiavari chairs and décor included.

### [Granite Bay Golf Club](https://www.invitedclubs.com/clubs/granite-bay-golf-club/host-an-event) – Upscale country club

Price: $900–2,750 rental; ceremony $550–1,100; catering ~$66 pp+.  
Capacity: 250 ballroom, 250 garden ceremony.  
Summary: Conservatory ballroom with skylights, wrap‑around porch; high‑end service.

### [Catta Verderea Country Club](https://www.cattaverdera.com/weddings) – Spanish‑style club (Lincoln)

Price: Site ≈ $3–4 K; packages from ~$9,600+.  
Capacity: 180.  
Summary: Mission‑style courtyard, wrought‑iron accents, full‑service packages in scenic foothills.

# Outdoor Wedding Venues near Roseville CA
