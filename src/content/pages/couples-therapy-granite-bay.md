## My Approach

I work from a differentiation-based model, grounded in the work of David Schnarch. This means we focus on individual growth within the relationship, not just communication techniques.

Most relationship problems stem from two people who haven't fully developed themselves. When each partner becomes more grounded and self-aware, the dynamic between you changes.
