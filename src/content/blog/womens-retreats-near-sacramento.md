## **Wellness & Renewal**

**HER weekend (Healing, Empowerment, Release)**  
The HER Weekend is a 2 1/2 day immersive, transformative and healing experience created for women ready to heal traumas and rediscover their power, their authentic purpose and what's truly possible. This is your chance to reflect on the life you've built and intentionally heal and shape what comes next.

  
I know and trust some of the people who lead this retreat.  
  
Locations vary. The most recent one I know of was held in Southern California.  
[Learn More](https://herweekend.com)

**Woman Within**  
The Woman Within Weekend is a two-and-a half-day experience for women. It provides a safe environment to explore how your life could be different and to fully know and live the power of who you are. It’s an opportunity to look at the life you’ve created and to make choices about what you want to sustain, what you want to deepen, and what you want to release.  
Locations vary.  
[Learn More](https://www.womanwithin.org/programs/woman-within-weekend)
